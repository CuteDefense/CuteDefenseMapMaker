Mirror
Enabling a mirror copies whichever half has more walls onto the other half. You'll get a prompt telling you which way before it changes anything.
Map
—
Pathing checks two phases. Red & blue flowers are walls only in their own phase, so a room walled with both keeps a door open each phase. Tall shrubs & yellow flowers always block. A 2-tile map border is unbuildable; Versus mode splits the field into two mirrored lanes.

Controls
Left-drag paint / erase / draw a room.
Right or middle drag (or Space+drag) to pan.
Wheel zooms toward the cursor. Click the minimap to jump.
Keys: 1-4 tiles, B/R/E/H tools, G grid, M minimap, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo, Ctrl+S save.
